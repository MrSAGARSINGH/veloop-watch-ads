# 🚀 VELOOP Rewards — Watch Ads

### Premium Watch Ads Experience

A modern, responsive, and interactive reward-based advertising dashboard built as part of the **VELOOP Rewards Frontend Internship Assignment**.

VELOOP Rewards transforms short advertisement sessions into an engaging earning experience where users can discover advertisements, watch them completely, earn **VEs**, track daily progress, explore rewards, and review their earning activity through a polished dashboard.



This makes the demo experience feel consistent across sessions while keeping the project completely frontend-focused.

> No backend authentication or server-side database is required for this frontend assignment implementation.

# 🎨 Design Philosophy

The interface was designed around three primary principles.

## 01 — Discover

Make advertisements, rewards, duration, and categories immediately understandable.

## 02 — Engage

Use clear actions, progress feedback, countdown states, and subtle animations to create an engaging watching experience.

## 03 — Reward

Keep earnings, progress, bonuses, and activity visible so users always understand the value of their actions.

```text
Discover
   ↓
Watch
   ↓
Complete
   ↓
Earn
   ↓
Track
   ↓
Repeat
```

---

# 🔍 UX Principles

### Clear Hierarchy

Important earning information is visually prioritized.

### Minimal Friction

Users can understand what to do without unnecessary steps.

### Immediate Feedback

Actions produce visible progress and state changes.

### Consistency

Cards, buttons, spacing, typography, and interactions follow a consistent design language.

### Responsive First

The experience adapts to different viewport sizes instead of simply shrinking the desktop layout.
